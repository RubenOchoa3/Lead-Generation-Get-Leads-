The 5-Minute Clean Test

7 spots most cleaning crews skip. Check yours before their next visit.

✗ if it fails
1Under the toilet rim and behind the baseTest: Shine your phone light under the rim.Fail: Stains, hair or buildup.
2Door handles and light switchesTest: Wipe one with a white paper towel.Fail: It comes away gray.
3Air ventsTest: Look up.Fail: Dust lines or fuzz on the grille.
4Baseboards and cornersTest: Run a finger along one foot of baseboard.Fail: Dust on your finger.
5Inside the breakroom microwaveTest: Look at the ceiling inside it.Fail: Old splatter.
6Under a desk and behind the trash cansTest: Crouch and look.Fail: Crumbs, dust bunnies, old liners.
7The restroom smell, mid-morningTest: Walk in around 10 AM.Fail: It smells. That usually means drains and grout aren't really being cleaned.
